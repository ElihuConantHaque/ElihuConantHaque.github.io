Elihu S. Conant-Haque

In collaboration with Mr. Eason Zhang and the Crown Musical Instrument Factory, Elihu has developed an exclusive line of tubas and euphoniums. These are premium-quality instruments made-to-order by Mr. Zhang and his small team. Critical components such as valves and linkages were designed with the help of B-Brass in Fiumicino, Italy. After being play-tested by Elihu, each horn is cleaned and adjusted by Jon Baltimore, New York City's most trusted woodwind-brasswind technician. Instruments are available for play-testing in the Lower East Side of Manhattan. Get in touch to try these excellent instruments for yourself!

Eason Zhang and the Crown Instrument Factory

Nestled in the beaucolic countryside of China's Shandong province, the Crown Instrument Factory is a bustling workshop that produces all types of wind and brass instruments. The team is led by Eason Zhang (张新星), a seasoned instrument builder and businessman. Elihu has made many trips to China to visit the factory, meet the craftsmen, and advise the team as they strive to improve design and production quality. Mr. Zhang has become a dear friend, and his work never ceases to impress.
