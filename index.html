Elihu S. Conant-Haque

Performer and Composer

Elihu S. Conant-Haque is a classically trained tuba player and composer living in NYC. He has won major solo competitions and been featured in a wide variety of music programs such as TubaBach, MostlyModern, LongPlay, and New Colossus. His compositions have premiered at major venues such as EMPAC and Zankel Hall, and often appear in contemporary film/multimedia projects. Elihu's musical interests are diverse. He brings a bold non-traditional approach to the tuba, incorporating styles from jazz bebop to Indonesian gamelan and championing the solo capabilities of the instrument.

Lessons

Elihu offers lessons in instrumental technique (tuba/euphonium) and music theory (composing/arranging) for the intermediate-to-advanced student. A course of study with Elihu is recommended for aspirational, self-motivated young musicians who will take an active role in their learning. His teaching style is systematic and conceptual; lessons involve more talking and less playing. Traditional exercises such as Bordogni études are deemphasized. Instead, students are encouraged to seek out music that they want to play, which promotes positive feelings about practicing. Schedule your free introductory lesson below!
